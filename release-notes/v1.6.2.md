### New
- **PS5 audio on your PC.** Open the new **BossQuickDock Receiver** on a Windows PC on the same network, press **Find PS5**, then **Listen on PC**. The console's own Opus encoder (48 kHz stereo, 10 ms packets) sends the sound over the LAN; **Back to Bluetooth** gives it back to your headset. Same look as the control page, with the instructions on screen. [Guide](https://github.com/ramalhoboss/BossQuickDock/blob/main/docs/pc-receiver.md)
- **Recording:** native AAC or Opus, 12 / 30 / 60 s, download as ADTS / Ogg or raw packets.
- **Diagnostics studio** on the control page: queue and bitpool charts, capture health, 1/5/10-minute stability tests, JSON report.
- **Readability:** Standard, Large and TV text modes; panels scroll instead of shrinking; **Stop app** always in the header.

### Fixes
- **Sound that went silent until a restart.** The console's capture library hands over one whole packet per read and, when the packet is larger than the read buffer, returns the same error forever. The buffer is now 64 KB (was 8 KB). Found by reverse engineering the library — [details](https://github.com/ramalhoboss/BossQuickDock/blob/main/docs/avcap2-internals.md).
- **Capture recovers by itself:** bad reads, a lost mapping or 5 s without sound reopen the capture (3 attempts, paced), and the status says why if it gives up.
- **Smoother Bluetooth pacing** when the queue drains after a stall.
- An experimental low-latency SBC path from the test builds dropped packets on the console (27 %); it is off, and SBC uses the proven paced queue (0.06 % in the release test).

### Install
Copy `BossQuickDock-1.6.2.elf` to `/data/pldmgr/payloads/local` and launch it from your payload manager. If an older BossQuickDock is running, press **Stop app** first.

Everything that changed since 1.1.2, with diagrams and measurements: [What's new in 1.6.2](https://github.com/ramalhoboss/BossQuickDock/blob/main/docs/whats-new-1.6.md).
