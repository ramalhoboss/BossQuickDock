### Fixes
- **SBC-XQ picked by hand no longer leaves you with dropouts.** When the link cannot carry SBC-XQ (still dropping at the bottom of the range for 10 s, or stuck under bitpool 30 for 30 s), BossQuickDock goes back to SBC by itself, shows SBC selected under System → Codec and says why. Before, this only happened when *Auto* had picked SBC-XQ.

### Good to know
- **Best quality on the PS5:** the console's Bluetooth controller pauses for 60–90 ms now and then, so SBC-XQ (~490 kbit/s) dropped ~340 packets a minute on a Sonic Sub 630 in our tests, while SBC at the speaker's maximum bitpool (39) played with no drops. Keep the codec on **Auto** or **SBC**: the bitpool climbs to the headset's maximum by itself and is remembered per headset. [Details and measurements](https://github.com/ramalhoboss/BossQuickDock/blob/main/docs/whats-new-1.6.md#163-the-best-bluetooth-quality)
- Everything from 1.6.2 is here: PS5 audio on your PC, native AAC / Opus recording, self-healing capture, diagnostics studio.

### Install
Copy `BossQuickDock-1.6.3.elf` to `/data/pldmgr/payloads/local` and launch it from your payload manager. If an older BossQuickDock is running, press **Stop app** first (otherwise the new one cannot start).
