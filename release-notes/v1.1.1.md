Bluetooth headphones and speakers on a jailbroken PS5, now also on **firmware 14.00 and 14.10** (tested on FW 13.60).

**All credit goes to X-F1REBALL-X** — this is a modified build of his [HearBridge PS5](https://github.com/X-F1REBALL-X/HearBridge-PS5). Thank you for releasing the source code! 🙏

### New
- **Firmware 14.00 / 14.10 support.** Built with PS5 Payload SDK v0.45, which adds the kernel offsets for these firmwares.
- **aptX codec** (optional): pick **aptX** under System → Codec. It only lights up when the headset supports aptX (48 kHz stereo). Auto stays SBC, and if the headset refuses aptX the app falls back to SBC on its own.
- **Low battery on screen:** a console notification at 20 % and 10 %, not only on the control page. "Connection lost" now names the headset.

### Fixes
- **Headphones and speakers pair on the first try much more often.** Sometimes the console's own Bluetooth took the "connected" message of the new link and hung up on it; the app then waited 45 s and the pairing failed. Now it pauses 2 s and connects again by itself (up to 2 times). The DualSense reconnect fix from 1.0.1 is unchanged.
- The codec buttons no longer offer SBC HQ / SBC-XQ to headsets that don't support them.

### Install
Copy `BossQuickDock-1.1.1.elf` to `/data/pldmgr/payloads/local` and launch it from your payload manager.
